Welcome to Kitala Senior Secndary School

Kitala Secondary School is a grant – aided government school.
Ministry registration and UNEB Center Number: U2712
It started in 2000 as a community school and as an Oꞌlevel school and was later upgraded to an Aꞌlevel secondary school in 2008.

LOCATION

The school is found in Busiro County, Katabi Town Council, Kitala Parish in Kitala village.
It is located 23km from Kampala city on the Kampala – Entebbe highway and less than a kilometre off the road that goes to Kitala Prisons.
It is directly opposite the Kitala Prisons and Kitala health center II and sits on 3.8 acres of land.
