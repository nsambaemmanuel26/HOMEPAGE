ABOUT KITALA S.S.S

Preamble:

Kitala secondary school is a grant – aided government school which started in 2000 as a community school.

It started as an Oꞌlevel school and was later upgraded to an Aꞌlevel secondary school in 2008.

Status:

Government aided, mixed school Ministry registration and UNEB Center Number U2712.

Historial Background and Foundation Body:

The foundation body is Kitala Town Council but earlier it was called Katabi Sub-County.

The school was founded as a community school by Katabi Town Council under the leadership
of Mr. Gerald Mukasa the chairperson LCIII.

It started with a small number of students but as the population grew, it operated under
double shift.

Later this was phased out after the construction of more buildings which were sponsored by the World Bank.

Infrastructure:

The old building was constructed by Katabi Town Council under the leadership of Gerald Mukasa.

Three classroom blocks and one laboratory block, one administrative block and two classroom shelters built on the newly acquired school land.

There is a kitchen for food preparation, a small temporary office block and toilets for boys and girls.

Three stances for boys and seven for girls.

The school is fenced with a perimeter wall and chain link.

Additional Information:

Kitala Senior Secondary School was started in 2008 by Katabi Local Government under the local government development project as a community mixed day school.

It started with senior one (S.1) and kept on upgrading classes up to senior six (S.6) in 2013.

From 2012 to 2014, it was under a double shift system due to inadequate infrastructure.

However, the government gave a grant which was used to put up more classrooms, two laboratories and an administration block.

It is located in Katabi Town Council, Wakiso district.

It is 23km from Kampala along Kampala – Entebbe road.

It is seated on 3.8 acres of land also opposite to Kitala prison and a health II.

It is a government day Oꞌ level and Aꞌ level school with an enrolment of 1200 students.

It has 48 teachers on government payroll, 4 non – teaching staff of which 2 are on government payroll and 5 support staff.

It has a functional Board of Governors appointed by government and a P.T.A executive.
